About The Symposium

Bridging Cutting-Edge Engineering with Climate-Resilient Urban Realities

The International Symposium on Sustainable Infrastructure, Transportation, Environment, Planning, and Architecture (IS-SITEPA 2026) is a premier interdisciplinary academic conference jointly organized by the Faculty of Engineering, Universitas Hasanuddin (FT UNHAS), Indonesia, and the Faculty of Civil Engineering (FKA) & Faculty of Built Environment and Surveying (FABU), Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM).

As climate volatility escalates across the Asia-Pacific and equatorial zones, civil infrastructure and urban habitats demand urgent transformation. IS-SITEPA 2026 unites academics, researchers, urban authorities, and industry pioneers to exchange breakthroughs in net-zero structural designs, electric transit systems, disaster-resilient water systems, and smart spatial planning.

Five Focused Scientific Tracks

Authors are invited to submit original research papers and case studies matching the symposium thematic pillars.

Track 01

Sustainable Infrastructure

  • Smart and low-carbon cementitious materials
  • Seismic retrofit & structural durability
  • Offshore & coastal infrastructure resilience
  • Digital twins and structural health monitoring
FT UNHAS & UTM FKA CODE: SI-01
Track 02

Transportation Systems

  • Electric vehicle charging infrastructure
  • Transit-Oriented Development (TOD)
  • Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) & AI
  • Maritime & intermodal logistics corridors
UTM FKA & FT UNHAS CODE: TS-02
Track 03

Environmental & Climate

  • Integrated watershed & urban flood modeling
  • Solid waste upcycling & circular economies
  • Carbon capture, utilization & storage (CCUS)
  • GIS and remote sensing for hazard mapping
Joint Department Committee CODE: EC-03
Track 04

Urban & Regional Planning

  • Smart sustainable cities and spatial policies
  • Informal settlements and inclusive regeneration
  • Land-use adaptation and food security zones
  • Regional governance & cross-border corridors
UTM FABU & FT UNHAS CODE: UP-04
Track 05

Sustainable Architecture

  • Passive climatic design & thermal performance
  • Vernacular wisdom & indigenous architecture
  • Net-Zero Energy Buildings (NZEB) standards

Host Destination

Makassar: Gateway to Eastern Indonesia

The symposium will take place at the iconic Grand Claro Hotel & Convention Center Makassar, South Sulawesi. Renowned for its state-of-the-art auditorium facilities, spacious banquet rooms, and prime downtown location near historical waterfronts.

Airport Proximity

25 minutes drive via toll road from Sultan Hasanuddin International Airport (UPG), offering non-stop flights to Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, and Jakarta.

Exclusive Accommodation Rate

Special negotiated rates available for IS-SITEPA participants at Grand Claro and neighboring 4-star hotels using booking code SITEPA26.
